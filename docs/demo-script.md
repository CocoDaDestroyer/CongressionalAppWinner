# CartWise demo script (about 90 seconds)

Record on a phone-sized browser window (390 x 844) in light mode. Before recording, open Profile and
press **Reset demo data** so every number below matches. Each line of narration is roughly the time
the shot stays on screen.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Home: the teal band, "The sticker price lies." | "Groceries cost more every month, and the sticker price doesn't tell you the real deal. CartWise does." |
| 0:08 | Tap **Scan or look up a product**. Heinz ketchup, 20 oz. The price tag prints in and the numeral rolls. | "Say I'm about to buy this 20-ounce Heinz." |
| 0:12 | Hold on the price tag with the yellow "Best per oz" tag: 64 oz, 14.0¢ /oz. | "CartWise compares every brand, size and nearby store per ounce. The 64-ounce bottle costs more on the shelf and is 30% cheaper per ounce." |
| 0:22 | Scroll the list slowly; pause on the Kroger row: faded ink, underlined price, COMMUNITY / STALE / UNVERIFIED stamps. | "Every price shows where it came from and how old it is. This one is a 45-day-old community report, so it's flagged." |
| 0:30 | Drag the slider to **lowest price**, then to **best reviews**. Watch the tag reprint. | "Care more about price or quality? Slide it, and the pick changes." |
| 0:36 | Set the slider back to the middle. Tap **Receipts**. Choose Heinz 64 oz, price 7.49, **Save receipt**. | "Receipts keep prices honest. I just paid $7.49 for that bottle." |
| 0:46 | The slip prints with a PRICES UPDATED stamp and the new receipt gets a highlight sweep. Tap **See it in Compare**. | "That receipt updated the price for everyone, and Compare already shows it." |
| 0:52 | Tap **Trip**. The total rolls in, the route draws itself, and "You save $3.60 vs. one store" sits on a yellow tag. | "Now the shopping list. CartWise picks which stores to visit and counts the gas." |
| 1:00 | Scroll to **Why this plan**. Point at the fuel segment on "Chase every lowest price". | "Chasing every lowest price saves a dollar on groceries and spends two on gas. The cheap store isn't worth the drive." |
| 1:10 | Tap the teal "Link your Ralphs and Target cards…" link. On Profile, switch on the Ralphs card. Go back to **Trip**. | "Link a store card and member prices count too. The trip just got cheaper." |
| 1:20 | Tap **Spending**. Saved-this-month is the hero number; the stores print as a receipt. | "And every receipt adds up to a picture of where your money goes." |
| 1:26 | Back to Home. | "CartWise: the real price, the right store, and prices kept honest by the people who shop there." |

## Optional: a real scan

On a phone served over https (or localhost), tap the camera button next to **Look up** and point it
at a Heinz 64 oz bottle (UPC 013000006415). Compare switches to it. Practice once before recording:
it needs good light.

## Shots to avoid

- Don't open the barcode field on camera unless you're showing the add-a-product flow. A typo shows
  an error.
- Keep the receipt date at its default (Aug 21, 2026). An older date is correctly outranked by
  newer prices, and the toast says it only counts toward spending.
